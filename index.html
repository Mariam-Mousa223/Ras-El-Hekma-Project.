FAQs

Where exactly is Ras El Hekma?

85 km west of Alexandria and 200 km east of Marsa Matrouh, on Egypt’s North Coast. The new international airport sits inside the city limits.

When does the airport open and which airlines will fly there?
  • The airport opens in phases starting Q4 2026.
  • Confirmed carries: Emirates, Etihad, Qatar Airways, Air Cairo, EgyptAir, Wizz Air, seasonal charters from Europe.
Can foreigners own property outright?

Yes. Egypt’s 2023 investment law allows freehold ownership for all nationalities, with title deeds registered in the buyer’s name.

Is there a residency or citizenship option?
  • Purchase ≥ US$300k and you qualify for Egypt’s Golden Residency (5-year renewable).
  • Purchase ≥ US$500k for fast-track citizenship (subject to due diligence).
What payment plans are available?
  • 0 % interest, 10 % down, balance over 8 years (most developers).
Are utilities and infrastructure already in?

Yes. Smart-grid electricity, fiber-optic internet, district cooling, desalinated water, and waste-water treatment are under construction simultaneously with the buildings.

Which communities are already under construction and when will they deliver?
  • Mountain View, Solare, Seashore, and Fouka Bay are under piling; the first units' handover is in Q3 2027.
  • NAIA Bay, Cali Coast & Masyaf are in earthworks; handover Q1 2028.
Can I rent my unit when I'm not there?

Yes. All developments allow short-term holiday lets; average gross yield 8 to 12 % net of service charges.

Are there schools and hospitals?
  • 4 million m² reserved for education: British, IB, and American curricula opening 2028 to 29.
  • A 400-bed Cleveland Clinic-affiliated hospital plus 6 satellite clinics will serve the city.
How will I get around the city?
  • 12 km of electric tram loops, 85 km of cycling lanes, e-scooter share, and autonomous shuttles.
  • Free intra-city ferry service connects all four marinas.
Is financing available for Egyptian expats?

Yes, Banque Misr, NBE, and ADCB Egypt provide up to 80 % LTV for expats at 7.5 % fixed for 15 years.

What climate should I expect?

Mediterranean: 30 °C summers, 15 °C winters, 320 sunny days a year, moderated by on-shore breezes.
